## Severity definitions

- **P0:** blocks honest paid public release, security/privacy boundary or complete source audit.
- **P1:** significant task or conversion risk; must be fixed before a trustworthy public launch.
- **P2:** visible quality and fit-and-finish issue, schedulable after critical failures.

## Score interpretation

8–10 requires operational proof and reviewed legal/claims; 5–7 means coherent draft but unclosed launch gates; below 5 means material strategic incompleteness. These are evidence-based editorial ratings, not standardized metrics.
