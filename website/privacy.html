Privacy & ethics

What we record. What we never record. What happens to your data.

Use only where you are allowed to. This page states the facts plainly — including the parts that are still undecided, marked as such.

The short version

What is recorded

  • Your confirmed profile facts — the ones you approved, stored encrypted on your PC.
  • Live transcripts — the interviewer’s questions, transcribed live, kept on your PC by default.
  • Reports — questions asked and suggestions shown, on your PC by default.
  • Usage ledger — server-side metering of live minutes, for fair billing only.
  • Payment references — with Paddle only; the app and backend never see card data.

The short version

What is never recorded

  • Your microphone is not recorded. The app listens to the interviewer’s audio from your headset; your mic is not a source.
  • Audio is never stored. It is transcribed live and never written to disk.
  • No hidden mode. The app is a normal, visible window. It never hides, never evades screen capture, and never pretends to be something else.
  • No hiring scores or predictions. There is nothing to record about an outcome the app does not predict.

Sign-in happens in your system browser — Google, Microsoft, or an email link. The service holds the AI key; you never paste a key yourself. Developer mode (your own OpenAI key) exists for internal testing and is not a selling point of this site.

Your rights

Export, delete, and ask

You can export or delete your workspace from the app. Account-owned documents can be exported and deleted; a deletion request is not replaced with an unconditional success message if part of it is still pending — you see a scoped pending/export/erase status instead. Paddle invoices sit outside app deletion: deleting your app data does not delete Paddle’s own records, which follow Paddle’s legal retention.

To request your data or its deletion once the support channel opens, see the privacy-request runbook summary on the Support page. The support channel is still TBD — we do not invent a contact address here.

Consent and ethics

Live assist is for interviews where assistance is permitted. Always check the interview’s rules — and the employer’s — first. The app is a preparation and practice coach; it does not help you deceive anyone, and it must not be used to present experience that is not yours. Only facts you confirmed are ever presented as your experience.
