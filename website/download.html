Two ways to run it

Installer

Per-user install, no admin needed. The installer will be code-signed, and its SHA-256 hash will be published on this page so you can verify the file you downloaded is the file we released.

Portable ZIP

Unzip and run, no install. Same app, same features — for machines where you cannot or do not want to install anything.

You’ll need

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • A headset — the app listens to the interviewer’s audio from your headset (WASAPI loopback)
  • An internet connection for live transcription and AI answers. Prepared answers keep working offline with a cached entitlement, and your reports stay on your PC.

Installing

  1. Download the signed installer (when published) and check its SHA-256 against the hash on this page.
  2. Run it — per-user install, no admin needed.
  3. Sign in in your browser (Google, Microsoft, or an email link).
  4. Import your resume, confirm the facts, and run your first preparation.

What happens after you install

Your first run, step by step. This describes the first signed release — the installer is not published yet, so these steps are what to expect, not something you can do today.

  1. Install — run the signed installer and check its SHA-256 against the hash published on this page.
  2. Sign in — in your browser: Google, Microsoft, or an email link.
  3. Import your resume — PDF, DOCX, or TXT.
  4. Confirm the facts — review every extracted fact; only facts you confirm are ever used.
  5. Add the job description — the app builds your 50–150 question bank for that interview.
  6. Go live — start listening with Ctrl+Alt+L; three points appear as each question ends.

You will need: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), a headset, and an internet connection for live transcription — see the list above.

Updates

Updates are delta-based (only the changed parts download) and they never install during an interview. The app checks for updates between sessions, not while you are live.
