Features

Everything it does, and the evidence for each claim.

Interview Assistant is a preparation and practice coach first, and a live assist tool second. Every feature below traces to the source product — the claim table in our planning docs lists the source for each one. Where something is internal test evidence rather than a guarantee, we say so.

1 · Prepare

A question bank built for this interview, not a generic list

Import your resume (PDF, DOCX or TXT) and the job description. The app extracts facts about your experience — and then you review every single one. Only facts you confirm are ever used. Nothing inferred, nothing guessed, nothing presented as your experience unless you said so.

From your confirmed facts and the role, a 15-stage preparation pipeline builds a bank of 50–150 questions for that interview: role-specific, behavioural, and follow-up questions, matched to what the job actually asks for.

Questions are classified as Verified (grounded in a confirmed fact), Hypothetical (a reasonable scenario to practise), or Bridge (connecting your experience to the role) — so you always know which answers rest on your real history and which are practice.

What the pipeline does with your resume

  • Import — PDF, DOCX or TXT resume, plus the job description.
  • Extract — facts, roles, results, and skills, as candidates — not as truth.
  • Confirm — you approve, edit, or reject every fact before it is used.
  • Match — the role and job description shape which questions matter.
  • Build — a 50–150 question bank, classified Verified / Hypothetical / Bridge.
  • Practise — work through the bank before the day, and review afterwards.

Preparation runs are metered: 2 per month on Trial, 20 per month on Pro. Preparation itself never uses live minutes.

2 · Live

Three short, speakable points — when the question ends

In the interview, the app listens to the interviewer’s audio from your headset (WASAPI loopback on Windows — no browser extension, no meeting-app plugin). It transcribes the question as it streams, detects the end of the question, and merges multi-part questions into one.

Then it shows three short, speakable bullets, one at a time. Short enough to say in your own words — not a script to read out. Every point is built only from facts you confirmed and your prepared question bank.

Try it in the simulated demo →

Coach Mode

Prefer a nudge over full points? Coach Mode shows three keywords and a one-line structure instead — so the words stay yours. Toggle it live, mid-interview, with Ctrl+Alt+C.

Coach Mode is included in both Trial and Pro.

It adapts when you’re interrupted

If the interviewer follows up fast, the next answer shortens automatically — Concise, then Rapid — so you can keep up. Reset it any time with Ctrl+Alt+R.

4 · Report

An honest report. No scores, no hiring predictions.

After you stop, you get the questions that were asked and the suggestions that were shown — plus what to prepare next time. That is all. There is no score, no grade, no pass/fail, and no prediction about whether you will be hired, because the app does not know that and never claims to.

Reports stay on your PC by default. Trial reports are kept 14 days; Pro reports 365 days.

5 · Privacy, built in

A visible window. Nothing hidden, nothing stored.

Interview Assistant is a normal, visible window. It never hides, never evades screen capture, and never pretends to be something else. It does not record your microphone, and it does not store audio — audio is transcribed live and never written to disk.

Your workspace is encrypted on your PC (DPAPI). Payment data lives with Paddle only. Read the full privacy page →
