What happens when…

When reality interrupts the script

The trace is the clean run; interviews are not clean. Four things that actually happen — and what the app does about each:

The interviewer interrupts

The next answer shortens automatically — Concise, then Rapid — so you can keep up. Reset any time with Ctrl+Alt+R and the full points come back.

You stop early

Stopping is yours: press stop and the app stops listening, there and then. The report compiles what actually happened — a short interview is still a complete record.

The minutes question

Minutes run only while you listen live. The Trial gives you 30 live minutes, once, for the life of the account. Pro gives you 600 per billing period. Practise and preparation never touch them.

Afterwards

The report lists questions asked, suggestions shown and what to prepare next — no scores, no hiring predictions. It stays on your PC by default, kept 14 days on Trial and 365 on Pro.

First run

From install to interview, once

Before the day

  1. Install the Windows app (per-user, no admin needed).
  2. Sign in in your browser — Google, Microsoft, or an email link.
  3. Import your resume and confirm the facts it finds.
  4. Add the role and the job description.
  5. Run preparation and get your 50–150 question bank.

On the day

  1. Practise a few questions out loud, in Answer or Coach Mode.
  2. Go live — only where you are allowed to — with 30 free Trial minutes.

Practise flow

Practise like it’s the day

Practise uses the same bank and answer shapes as live mode, so nothing on the day surprises you. Pick a question, answer aloud, compare with the three points the app would have shown, move on. The questions you struggled with are the ones to fold into your next preparation run.

Practise and preparation never use live minutes — only live listening does. See how live minutes work.
