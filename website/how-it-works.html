  1. 01 · Profile

    Import your resume. Confirm the facts.

    What happens: you import a resume (PDF, DOCX or TXT) and the app extracts facts — roles, results, skills — as candidates.

    What you do: review every extracted fact. Approve it, edit it, or reject it. Only confirmed facts are ever used to build answers.

    What you see: a profile you recognise as your own — because you built it, fact by fact. Sign-in is via your browser (Google, Microsoft, or an email link); the app holds a service AI key, so you never paste a key yourself.

  2. 02 · Prepare

    Add the role and job description. Get your question bank.

    What happens: the 15-stage preparation pipeline matches your confirmed facts to the role and builds a bank of 50–150 questions — role-specific, behavioural, and follow-ups — classified Verified, Hypothetical, or Bridge.

    What you do: work through the bank. Practise the questions you are weakest on. Re-run preparation for a different role — 2 runs a month on Trial, 20 on Pro.

    What you see: a bank organised by question type, with the source fact behind each Verified question. Preparation never uses live minutes.

  3. 03 · Live

    Start listening. Three points appear as questions end.

    What happens: with a headset on, the app captures the interviewer’s audio (WASAPI loopback), transcribes it as it streams, detects the end of each question, and shows three short, speakable bullets one at a time — built only from your confirmed facts.

    What you do: speak in your own words, using the points as a guide — or switch to Coach Mode for keywords and a structure line instead. If the interviewer interrupts, answers shorten automatically (Concise, then Rapid).

    What you see: a small, visible window. It never hides. It never records your microphone and never stores audio.

  4. 04 · Report

    Stop, review, and prepare better next time.

    What happens: when you stop the session, the app compiles an honest report: the questions asked, the suggestions shown, and what to prepare next time.

    What you do: read it, mark the questions you struggled with, and fold them into your next preparation run.

    What you see: a record of what happened — no scores, no hiring predictions. Reports stay on your PC by default (14 days on Trial, 365 days on Pro).

First run

Your first hour with the app

  1. Install the Windows app (per-user, no admin needed).
  2. Sign in in your browser — Google, Microsoft, or an email link.
  3. Import your resume and confirm the facts it finds.
  4. Add the role and the job description.
  5. Run preparation and get your 50–150 question bank.
  6. Practise a few questions out loud, in Answer or Coach Mode.
  7. Go live — only where you are allowed to — with 30 free Trial minutes.

Practise flow

Practise like it’s the day

Practise uses the same question bank and the same answer shapes as the live mode, so nothing on the day surprises you. Pick a question, answer out loud, compare with the three points the app would have shown, and move on. The questions you mark as weak come back first next time.

Practise and preparation do not use live minutes — only live listening sessions do. See how live minutes work.

Hotkeys, in the app

Ctrl+Alt+L start / pause live listening
Ctrl+Alt+C Answer / Coach Mode
Ctrl+Alt+R reset adaptive shortening
Ctrl+Alt+Space show / hide the window
