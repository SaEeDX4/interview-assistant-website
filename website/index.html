What it does

Prepared before. Steady during. Honest after.

▤

Prepare from your resume

Import your resume and the job description. Review every extracted fact — only facts you confirm are ever used. A 50–150 question bank is built for that interview.

◉

Three points, live

When a question ends, three short, speakable bullets appear one at a time. Short enough to say — not to read out.

✦

Coach Mode

Prefer a nudge over a script? Coach Mode shows three keywords and a one-line structure, so the words stay yours. Toggle it live with Ctrl+Alt+C.

🌐

Ten languages, including RTL

Answers follow the interviewer’s language. Arabic and Persian render right-to-left, properly — not as an afterthought.

↯

Adapts when you’re interrupted

A fast follow-up shortens the next answer automatically — Concise, then Rapid — so you can keep up. Reset it with one hotkey.

✓

An honest report

Afterwards, see the questions asked and the suggestions shown. No scores, no hiring predictions — just what happened, so you can prepare better next time.

FAQ

The hard questions, answered plainly

Is it allowed to use Interview Assistant in an interview?

Only where you are allowed to. It is a preparation and practice coach with a live assist mode for interviews where assistance is permitted. Always check the interview’s rules — and the employer’s — first. The app is a visible window; it never hides.

What does it record?

It does not record your microphone, and it does not store audio. It listens to the interviewer’s audio from your headset to transcribe questions live. Transcripts and reports stay on your PC by default.

Does it work in my language?

It supports 10 languages, including Persian and Arabic with proper right-to-left display. Answers follow the interviewer’s language. Language support is as listed in the app; live quality is being validated language by language.

Does it work offline?

Live transcription and AI answers need an internet connection. Prepared answers keep working offline with a cached entitlement, and your reports stay on your PC.

How do I cancel Pro?

Cancel anytime — you keep Pro until the end of your billing period. Paid checkout is not open yet; the Pro price shown is a Paddle sandbox reference, and the final live price will be confirmed before checkout opens.

What does it cost?

The Trial gives you 30 lifetime live minutes, 1 profile, and 2 preparation runs a month. Pro is USD 19.99 a month for 600 live minutes per billing period, 3 profiles, and 20 preparation runs. Billed by Paddle — no card details ever touch the app.

What happens if the interviewer interrupts me?

The next answer gets shorter automatically (Concise, then Rapid), so you can keep up. You can reset it with Ctrl+Alt+R.

What is Coach Mode?

Instead of full bullets, Coach Mode shows three keywords and a one-line structure, so you speak in your own words. Toggle it live with Ctrl+Alt+C.

What do I get after the interview?

An honest report: the questions asked and the suggestions shown, plus what to prepare next time. No scores, no hiring predictions.

Ethics & privacy

Use only where you are allowed to.

Visible, always

Interview Assistant is a normal, visible window. It never hides, never evades screen capture, and never pretends to be something else.

Your audio is not stored

It does not record your microphone. Audio is transcribed live and never written to disk. Transcripts and reports stay on your PC by default.

Your facts are yours

Only facts you confirmed are ever presented as your experience. Your workspace is encrypted on your PC; you can export or delete it. Payment data lives with Paddle only.
