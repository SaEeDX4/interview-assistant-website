1 · The service

Interview Assistant is a Windows interview preparation and practice coach, with a live assist mode for interviews where assistance is permitted. The service is provided as described on this site; features marked as planned or in research are not part of the service until they ship.

2 · Plans and live minutes

Trial — $0, 30 lifetime live minutes, 1 candidate profile, 2 preparation runs per month, 1 device, reports kept 14 days. Pro — USD 19.99 per month (sandbox reference price; final live price to be confirmed before paid checkout opens), 600 live minutes per billing period, 3 profiles, 20 preparation runs per month, 2 devices, reports kept 365 days. Live minutes are minutes spent in a live listening session, metered by the server. Preparation, practise, and reports do not use live minutes. Safety caps apply: 240 live minutes per account per day, 120 minutes per single live session.

3 · Authorised use

You may use live assist only where you are allowed to — where the interview’s rules and the employer’s rules permit it. You must not use the service to misrepresent your experience: only facts you confirmed may be presented as yours. You must not use the service to hide from screen capture, to record anyone without permission, or to evade any interview rule. The app is a visible window and does not offer a hidden mode; any claim otherwise is false.

4 · Your account and your data

You sign in via your browser (Google, Microsoft, or an email link). You are responsible for your account and for the accuracy of the facts you confirm. Your workspace is encrypted on your PC; you can export or delete it. See the Privacy page for the data inventory, retention, and erasure rules.

5 · Payment, cancellation, refunds

Payment is handled by Paddle, the Merchant of Record. Card data never touches the app or the backend; checkout and the customer portal are Paddle-hosted pages. Paid checkout is not open yet, so there is no live subscription to cancel. When it opens, you can cancel in the Paddle portal and keep Pro until the end of your paid period, then fall back to Trial. Refunds are handled by Paddle as Merchant of Record; the final Terms will state the refund rules that Paddle’s terms allow us to state. Downgrades will go through the Paddle portal once paid checkout opens; the effective date is the one shown there.

6 · What the service does not do

The service does not score you, grade you, predict hiring outcomes, or guarantee that you will pass an interview. Reports are a record of what happened — questions asked and suggestions shown — not an assessment. The service does not record your microphone or store audio.

7 · Availability and changes

The service may change: features may be added, changed, or removed, and prices may change with notice. The final Terms will state how much notice price changes get. Planned platforms (Android, iOS, Mac) are not commitments and carry no dates.

8 · Termination

We may suspend or terminate accounts that misuse the service — including unauthorised use in interviews, misrepresentation, or abuse of the metering caps. You may stop using the service at any time and delete your workspace. The final Terms will state the termination notice and appeal process.

9 · Contact

The support channel is still to be decided (see Support). The final Terms will name the provider’s legal name and a contact address. Until then, do not treat any address on this site as the legal contact for the service.
