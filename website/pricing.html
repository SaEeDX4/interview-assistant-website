Live minutes, explained

What a “live minute” actually is

A live minute is a minute you spend in a live listening session — the time the app is actively listening and transcribing in an interview. Minutes are metered by the server (a heartbeat every 30 seconds, with billed seconds per heartbeat capped at twice the interval, so sleep or offline time is not billed).

Preparation, practise, and reading reports do not use live minutes at all — they have their own quota (preparation runs per month, above).

Safety caps

To stop abuse and runaway cost, the service caps live use at 240 live minutes per account per day and 120 minutes per single live session. These are operator-level caps, not selling points — but we state them honestly so there are no surprises.

What happens when minutes run out

When your Trial’s 30 lifetime minutes are used, live listening stops until you move to Pro. Your prepared question banks and reports stay available. Upgrading to Pro resets live minutes to the Pro allowance for the billing period, once payment is processed.

Billing

Billed by Paddle — the Merchant of Record

Paddle collects the payment, calculates and remits VAT and sales tax, issues invoices, and handles refunds and chargebacks. Card data never touches the desktop app or the backend — checkout and the customer portal are Paddle-hosted pages opened in your browser.

Cancel, downgrade, refunds

  • Cancel in the Paddle customer portal. You keep Pro until the end of your paid period — access is not cut off immediately — then fall back to Trial.
  • Downgrade (Pro → Trial) also goes through the Paddle portal; the exact effective date is the one shown in the portal — we do not invent a downgrade date here.
  • Refunds are handled by Paddle as Merchant of Record. Refund terms will be stated in the final Terms; the draft on this site points to Paddle for the same reason — we do not write refund rules we cannot enforce.

Pricing FAQ

Is the Trial really free?

Yes — $0, with 30 lifetime live minutes, 1 profile, and 2 preparation runs a month. No card is needed for the Trial.

Do unused Pro minutes roll over?

The plan catalog gives 600 live minutes per billing period. The source does not state a rollover rule, so we do not claim one — the final Terms will say so explicitly.

Can I use Pro on two computers?

Yes — Pro allows 2 devices; Trial allows 1.

What if I only interview once a month?

Then the Trial’s 30 lifetime minutes may be enough for a while. Pro is for a serious job search — several interviews a month, several roles, several profiles.

When can I actually pay?

Not yet. Paid checkout opens only after the Paddle live price is confirmed and the checkout flow is tested end to end. Until then, the price on this page is a sandbox reference, and the Trial is the way in.
