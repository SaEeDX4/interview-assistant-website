Live minutes, explained

What a “live minute” actually is

A live minute is a minute you spend in a live listening session — the time the app is actively listening and transcribing in an interview. Live minutes are metered by our server, as the Terms (§2) put it.

Preparation, practise, and reading reports do not use live minutes at all — they have their own quota (preparation runs per month, above).

Safety caps

To stop abuse and runaway cost, the service caps live use for every account — Trial and Pro alike. These are operator-level caps, not selling points — but we state them honestly so there are no surprises.

240

live minutes per account per day — reaching the daily cap pauses live assist until the next day

120

minutes in a single live session — a session that reaches the cap ends there

What happens when minutes run out

When your Trial’s 30 lifetime minutes are used, live listening stops until you move to Pro. Your prepared question banks and reports stay available. When you move to Pro, the Pro allowance — 600 minutes per billing period — applies from that period.

Billing

Billed by Paddle — the Merchant of Record

Paddle collects the payment, calculates and remits VAT and sales tax, issues invoices, and handles refunds and chargebacks. Card data never touches the desktop app or the backend — checkout and the customer portal are Paddle-hosted pages opened in your browser.

Cancel, downgrade, refunds

None of this can happen yet — paid checkout is not open, so there is no live subscription to cancel and nobody has been charged. When paid plans open, these are the rules, exactly as the Terms (§5) state them:

  • Cancel — at any time in the Paddle customer portal. You keep Pro until the end of your paid period — access is not cut off immediately — no further payments are taken, and your account then falls back to Trial.
  • Downgrade (Pro → Trial) also goes through the Paddle portal, and takes effect on the date shown there — we do not invent a downgrade date here.
  • Refunds — statutory rights first. Nothing here limits any refund, withdrawal, or cancellation right that the law or Paddle’s Buyer Terms give you; where they give you more than this policy, they prevail.
  • First payment. Ask within 14 days of your first Pro payment and that payment is refunded in full.
  • Renewals. Fees for a billing period that has already begun are not refundable, except where the law requires it.
  • If we end it. If we discontinue the service, or terminate your account other than for a breach of the Terms, the unused part of your current paid period is refunded, pro-rated.
  • How refunds are made. Request one through Paddle (the link on your receipt, or paddle.net) or by emailing support@cybervalida.online. Approved refunds are issued by Paddle to the original payment method — and a refund ends the Pro access it paid for.
