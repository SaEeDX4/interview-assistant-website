Support & FAQ

Help, troubleshooting, and the hard questions.

Start with the FAQ — it answers the questions people actually ask, including the uncomfortable ones. If you are stuck on something technical, the troubleshooting sections below are written from the product’s own support playbooks.

How to reach us: the support channel is still to be decided — there is no support email yet, and we will not invent one. When it opens, this page will name the channel, its hours, and what to include in a report. Until then, the troubleshooting below covers the common problems.

Troubleshooting

Audio

No audio / wrong playback device

The app captures the interviewer’s audio from your headset via WASAPI loopback. If nothing is transcribed: check that the headset is the default playback device in Windows sound settings, that the meeting audio is actually playing through it (not your speakers), and that no other app has exclusive control of the device. If the stream is empty, the app records that as no audio — an empty stream is not a pass, and it will not invent a transcript.

Audio

The app hears the wrong app

WASAPI loopback captures what plays through the selected playback device. If music or another call is playing through the same headset, it is in the stream. Close other audio sources before the interview, and use a headset — not speakers — so your own voice does not feed back.

Account

Sign-in problems

Sign-in happens in your system browser (Google, Microsoft, or an email link). If sign-in fails, try a fresh browser sign-in, check that your default browser can reach the sign-in page, and retry. Prepared answers keep working offline with a cached entitlement, so a sign-in problem does not erase your preparation.

Billing

Minutes running out

Live minutes are metered by the server. If live listening stops, check how many live minutes you have used — Trial gives 30 lifetime minutes. Preparation and practise do not use live minutes, so a “minutes used up” message only ever concerns live sessions. See how live minutes work.

Download

Download problems

There is no installer yet — the signed installer and its SHA-256 hash are not published. If a link on this site does not download anything, that is expected for now, not a bug. See Download.

Privacy-request runbook (summary)

When the support channel opens, a privacy request (export or erasure) will follow these steps, from the product’s own runbook:

  1. You submit the request through the support channel.
  2. Your identity is verified, so nobody can request your data.
  3. The request is traced across the local workspace, the backend, and Paddle, field by field.
  4. Export or erasure is carried out, with Paddle invoices explicitly outside app deletion.
  5. You receive a scoped confirmation — what was done, and what is still pending if anything is.

A partial removal is never presented as a complete one.
